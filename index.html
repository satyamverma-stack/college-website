About Babu Sunder Singh Degree Collage


Babu Sunder Singh Degree College, Lucknow was established in 2022 under the Babu Sunder Singh Foundation and is affiliated with Lucknow University. The college offers undergraduate programs such as BCA, BBA, and B.Com, with a strong focus on academic excellence, practical learning, skill development, and ethical values. The institution is committed to preparing students for successful careers by combining quality education with industry-oriented training and holistic development. The college is located on a spacious 30-acre campus near NH-24B, Raebareli Road, Nigohan, Lucknow.

The campus is equipped with modern infrastructure, including smart classrooms, Wi-Fi connectivity, a fully automated library, separate hostels for boys and girls, sports facilities, and medical assistance for hostel residents. Through seminars, workshops, internships, and co-curricular activities, students are encouraged to develop leadership qualities, professional skills, and social responsibility.

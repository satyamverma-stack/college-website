About Us

Babu Sunder Singh Degree College, established in 2022 by the Babu Sunder Singh Foundation, is affiliated with Lucknow University and offers BBA, BCA, and BCom programs. Located on a 30-acre campus near NH-24B, the college is well-equipped with Wi-Fi, a fully automated library, modern classrooms, and separate hostels for boys and girls. It also features a sports complex and provides medical assistance to hostel residents. With a focus on academic excellence, practical learning, and ethical values, the college promotes industry interactions, co-curricular activities, and overall student development. Guided by Mr. Anand Shekhar Singh, the college aims to nurture skilled professionals in management, commerce, and computer applications.
